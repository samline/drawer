## Snap points

<Playground preset="snaps" showCode />

The points are ordered visible sizes. Numeric `1` means zero offset; fractional stops track the viewport. Pixel strings are also supported and use integer pixel counts. The handle cycles through the points. Learn about [gestures and snap points](../gestures-and-snap-points/).

## Nested flow

<Playground preset="nested" showCode />

The child is related with `parentId`. Closing it leaves its parent open; destroying the parent also destroys its child. Keep ids unique when using these examples together.

## Apply filters

<Playground preset="form" showCode />

A native labeled form reads its selected category with `FormData`, reports the result outside the drawer and calls `setOpen(false)`. It does not submit a network request.

## Scroll long content

<Playground preset="scroll" showCode />

The panel has its own scroll container and a sticky close button. Test wheel, keyboard and touch scrolling without moving the underlying page.

## Non-modal inspector

<Playground preset="nonmodal" showCode />

`modal: false` and `overlay: false` leave the background interactive. This intentionally does not trap focus or isolate the page; choose it for an inspector, not a blocking confirmation.

## Handle-only dragging

<Playground preset="handle" showCode />

`handleOnly: true` restricts dragging to the native handle. The textarea is also marked `data-drawer-no-drag`. This is useful for text selection, sliders and other content gestures.

## Update the same instance

<Playground preset="update" showCode />

The action replaces title and body through `update()`. The updated content remains when reopened. For a form or editor, keep application data outside disposable dialog DOM.

## Controlled snap size

<Playground preset="controlled" showCode />

Compact and Expanded call `setActiveSnapPoint()` with exact members of the configured array. Direct calls update the snapshot; they do not echo `onActiveSnapPointChange`.

## Cleanup

Copied pages preserve listeners during a persisted `pagehide` and destroy on a real departure. In an SPA, call `drawer.destroy()` when the feature unmounts and unsubscribe any application listeners. Never call `destroyDrawers()` from one feature if unrelated features still own drawers.
