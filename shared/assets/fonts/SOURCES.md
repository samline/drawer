# Font sources

Website WOFF2 files follow the pinned Ticker revision and the Fontsource provenance, versions, weights, sizes and hashes in README.md. Keep all three SIL Open Font License notices.

Inter.ttf and InstrumentSerif-Italic.ttf were extracted from the original licensed Ticker assets and remain build-only inputs for the generated social image. They are not copied to public folders or used as website downloads. The obsolete JetBrains Mono TTF was removed after migrating its only consumer, SiteHead, to Fonts/WOFF2.
